How the analysis-first 3D generation works

Stage 1 inspects the alpha channel before touching the image, removes a background/model only when needed, separates the garments, aligns front/back landmarks and checks for duplicate views. If a required view is missing, generation stays blocked until it is resolved. Continue then fits garment-specific hoodie/pants meshes over the mannequin, creates dedicated left/right side textures from edge seams and fabric information, keeps central logos/artwork on their intended panels, adds controlled cloth folds, and builds the front/back showcase motion. The export renderer temporarily switches to a native 7680x4320 drawing buffer for the 8K output, then restores the interactive viewer.
